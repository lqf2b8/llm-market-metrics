# Weekly metrics review: week of 2026-09-14

## Headline
Implied spend was $147.1M for the week starting 2026-09-14, a change of +12.2% from the prior week. Tokens were 128.9T, a change of +1.7%.

## What moved and why
Implied spend moved from $131.2M (week starting 2026-09-07) to $147.1M, a change of +$16.0M. Volume contributed +$2.2M. Mix contributed +$13.8M. Price contributed $0.

The price effect is $0 because of how the data is built. Daily price snapshots began on 2026-10-28, and earlier usage is priced at the first snapshot. The $0 is not evidence that prices were flat.

Effective paid price changed by +13.3% to $1.35 per million tokens. With the price effect at $0, that change came through mix.

OpenAI drove the move. Its spend changed by +$20.6M: mix contributed +$20.0M and volume contributed +$640.0K.

Tencent's spend changed by -$4.7M. Mix contributed -$5.0M and volume contributed +$324.4K.

DeepSeek's spend changed by +$1.6M. Mix contributed +$1.5M and volume contributed +$100.5K.

The data does not show why mix shifted.

## Against plan
The plan is the frozen statistical forecast. Ranges are 90% prediction intervals.

- **Implied spend:** The forecast made the week of 2026-09-07 was $134.2M, with a range of $106.9M to $168.6M. The actual was $147.1M, which is +9.6% versus forecast and inside the range.
- **Tokens:** The forecast made the week of 2026-09-07 was 138.4T, with a range of 116.2T to 164.9T. The actual was 128.9T, which is -6.9% versus forecast and inside the range.

Next week's forecasts, for the week of 2026-09-21:
- **Implied spend:** $156.3M, with a range of $124.4M to $196.4M.
- **Tokens:** 142.4T, with a range of 119.5T to 169.7T.

Track record one week ahead: implied spend had 45 weeks with a range, 86.7% of actuals landed inside it, and the average miss was 11.4%. Tokens had 73 weeks with a range, 93.2% landed inside it, and the average miss was 9.6%.

## Providers
OpenAI holds the most spend at $59.1M, or 40.1% of spend. Anthropic follows at $34.4M (23.4%), then Tencent at $14.8M (10.0%).

Paid tokens sit elsewhere. Among the providers listed by spend, DeepSeek holds the largest paid-token share at 29.1%. Z-ai holds 17.2%, Tencent 15.9% and OpenAI 13.8%.

The largest pricing-power gap is OpenAI. Its spend share is 40.1% against a paid-token share of 13.8%, a gap of +26.3 pp. DeepSeek is the reverse, with a gap of -23.9 pp. Anthropic's gap is +19.7 pp.

## What to watch next week
- New models made up 62.3% of top 50 tokens, and 3 models entered the top 50. Watch whether both hold.
- Provider token moves were sharp: OpenAI's tokens changed by -32.1% and DeepSeek's by +35.7%. Tencent's changed by -16.1%.
- OpenAI's token change sits alongside its large mix effect on spend. Watch whether the two move together again.

## Data notes
- Pricing coverage is 100.0%, and this week's spend figures are marked reliable.
- OpenRouter is one marketplace, not the whole market.
- Implied spend is tokens x list price with an assumed prompt/completion split. It is an index, not revenue. Shares and changes are robust to the split, but the level is not.
- Token counts use each provider's tokenizer and are not strictly comparable.
- Price effects are $0 by construction, as described above.
