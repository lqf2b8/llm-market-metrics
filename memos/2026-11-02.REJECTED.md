## Data notes
- OpenRouter is one marketplace, not the whole market.
- Implied spend is tokens times list price, with an assumed prompt/completion split. It is an index of spend at list prices. Shares and changes are robust to the split, but the level is not.
- Token counts use each provider's tokenizer and are not strictly comparable.
- Priced coverage is 99.8%, so this week's spend figures are reliable.
- Daily price snapshots began on 2026-10-28. Earlier usage is priced at the first snapshot, so price effects for weeks before then are $0 by construction, not evidence that prices were flat.
- Forecasts are frozen the week they are made and never revised.
