# Weekly metrics review: week of 2026-08-31

## Headline

Implied spend for the week of 2026-08-31 was $121.2M, up +21.7% from the prior week. Almost all of the increase came from mix, not volume. Data runs through 2026-09-06.

## What moved and why

Implied spend changed by +$21.6M, from $99.6M in the week of 2026-08-24 to $121.2M. Volume contributed +$1.4M. Mix contributed +$20.2M. The price effect is $0.

The price effect is $0 by construction, not because prices were flat. Daily price snapshots began on 2026-10-28, and earlier usage is priced at the first snapshot. Volume, mix and price add up to the spend change exactly.

Tencent, anthropic and xiaomi drove most of the move, almost entirely through mix:

- Tencent's spend changed by +$12.7M. Mix contributed +$12.6M and volume contributed +$62.9K.
- Anthropic's spend changed by +$8.0M. Mix contributed +$7.5M and volume contributed +$481.9K.
- Xiaomi's spend changed by -$5.6M. Mix contributed -$5.7M and volume contributed +$109.1K.

Openai's spend also changed by +$5.6M. The data does not show why mix shifted.
