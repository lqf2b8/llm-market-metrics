# Weekly metrics review: week of 2026-10-19

## Headline
Implied spend was $176.8M for the week starting 2026-10-19, a change of +11.2% from the prior week. Total tokens were 188.0T, a change of +14.6%.

## What moved and why
Implied spend changed by +$17.8M, from $159.0M in the week starting 2026-10-12 to $176.8M. Volume contributed +$24.9M. Mix contributed -$7.1M.

The price effect is $0. Daily price snapshots began on 2026-10-28, and earlier usage is priced at the first snapshot. The $0 is therefore by construction and is not evidence that prices were flat.

Anthropic, StepFun and OpenAI drove the move:

- **Anthropic** changed by +$16.6M. Volume contributed +$11.1M and mix contributed +$5.5M.
- **StepFun** changed by -$11.1M. Volume contributed +$2.6M and mix contributed -$13.7M. StepFun’s mix effect was larger than its whole change; volume partly offset it.
- **OpenAI** changed by +$5.8M. Volume contributed +$3.1M and mix contributed +$2.7M.

The data does not show why these providers moved.

## Providers
Anthropic holds the largest share of spend, at 49.2% ($87.1M), but only 9.6% of paid tokens. DeepSeek holds the largest share of paid tokens, at 39.2%, but only 8.6% of spend ($15.2M).

Anthropic has the largest pricing-power gap, with a gap of +39.6 pp between its spend share and its paid-token share. DeepSeek's gap runs the other way, at -30.7 pp. Z-ai also has a negative gap, at -8.7 pp, with a 3.9% spend share and a 12.6% paid-token share.

Paid tokens were 152.9T, a change of +6.8%. This is slower than total token growth.

## What to watch next week
- New models made up 71.6% of top-50 tokens, and 4 models entered the top 50. Watch whether new-model share holds.
- DeepSeek's tokens changed by +30.7%, the sharpest move among the largest providers by spend. Watch whether it continues.
- Google's tokens changed by -13.3%, the only decline among the largest providers by spend.

## Data notes
- Implied spend is an index of tokens at list prices with an assumed prompt/completion split. It is not revenue. Shares and changes hold up under the split, but the level does not.
- Priced coverage was 100.0%, so spend figures are reliable this week.
- OpenRouter is one marketplace, not the whole market.
- Token counts use each provider's own tokenizer and are not strictly comparable.
