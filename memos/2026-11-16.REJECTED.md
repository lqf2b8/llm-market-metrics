# Weekly metrics review: week of 2026-11-16

## Headline

Tokens were 314.9T in the week starting 2026-11-16, a change of +49.0% from the prior week. Implied spend was $171.6M, a change of -0.7%, because the effective paid price changed by -35.6% to $0.60 per million tokens.

## What moved and why

Implied spend went from $172.8M in the week starting 2026-11-09 to $171.6M, a change of -$1.1M. Implied spend is an index of spend at list prices.

The bridge splits the change into three effects:

- Volume contributed +$89.3M.
- Mix contributed -$85.9M.
- Price contributed -$4.5M.

Volume and mix almost cancelled. The data does not show why.

Anthropic changed by -$3.0M, with volume at +$41.9M and mix at -$44.9M. Its price effect was $0.

Moonshotai changed by -$3.6M. Volume contributed +$8.9M, mix contributed -$7.4M and price contributed -$5.1M. Its price effect is the largest provider price effect in the facts.

Google changed by +$9.5M, with volume at +$5.0M and mix at +$4.5M. StepFun also moved, changing by -$4.6M, mostly through mix at -$9.3M.
